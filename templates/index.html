      Evidence Quality & Methodology

      How does VerifiX AI assess evidence strength?

      Evidence strength is categorized qualitatively as Strong, Moderate, Limited, or Insufficient based on source independence, the presence of primary documentation, relevance to the exact claim, and the absence of unresolved material conflicts.

      What happens when credible sources disagree?

      Rather than forcing an unearned binary verdict or deciding by majority vote, the system surfaces a Material Conflict Warning, explains what is established versus what is disputed, and assigns an Unverified verdict.

      Is a lack of search results proof that a claim is false?

      No. Absence of evidence is not evidence of absence. Obscure, local, or emerging claims with no verified records are classified as Unverified with an Insufficient Evidence status, not automatically labeled Misleading.
